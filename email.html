What is RCMI?

RCMI (Resin Car Model Indonesia) is Indonesia's community of 1:64 resin car model collectors and makers. Resin models are made in small runs, often from hand-made moulds — sharp detail, limited numbers, and every caster has their own style. Together we share our collections, meet up, and trade.

  • Market — sell, swap or hunt for models, chat with sellers, and build your seller rating
  • Meetups & events — first to hear about RCMI gatherings

Apa itu RCMI?

RCMI (Resin Car Model Indonesia) adalah komunitas kolektor dan pembuat model mobil resin skala 1:64 di Indonesia. Model resin dibuat dalam jumlah terbatas, sering dari cetakan buatan tangan — detail tajam, jumlah terbatas, dan setiap caster punya gaya sendiri. Bersama kita berbagi koleksi, kopdar, dan jual-beli.
