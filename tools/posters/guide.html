Participant guide

RCMI Photo Contest

How to join

01

Register

  • Go to rcmi-website.web.app and sign in with Google.
  • Exclusive member: enter your RCMI phone number. New here? Fill in a short profile — your Associate Member account is active right away.
  • Open Menu › Contest, tick that you agree to the rules, then tap Register.
  • You get a 7-digit registration number on screen and by email. Max 100 participants — free entry.
02

Upload your photo up to 2 per person

  • On the contest page, choose your photo and add a title (caption optional), then tap Submit.
  • Any 1:64 model car — diecast or resin — shot by you. Light editing is fine, no AI-generated images.
  • Want to enter a second shot? Tap ＋ Add a 2nd photo. You can change or remove photos until registration closes.
03

Assessment

  • The jury scores every photo 1–10 on five criteria:
Lighting & MoodStorytellingCraftsmanshipTheme ×1.5Creativity
  • Public vote: 1 vote per account, not for your own photo.
  • Top 3 win cash prizes · one prize per person (your best photo counts).
