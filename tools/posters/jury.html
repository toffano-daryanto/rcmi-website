Jury guide

RCMI Photo Contest

How to judge

01

Sign in

Open rcmi-website.web.app and sign in with the Google account that has the Jury role.

02

Open the panel

Menu › Contest › RCMI Photo Contest 2026, then tap 📋 Jury & admin panel.

03

See the entries

Who registered and who uploaded, by registration number. Names are hidden so judging stays fair. Tap a photo to see it big.

04

Score every photo

Under Jury scoring, set each slider 1–10, add a note if you like (only admins see it), tap Save.

Lighting & MoodStorytellingCraftsmanshipTheme ×1.5Creativity
05

Check & update

Green border = scored. Yellow border = the photo was changed after you scored — please score it again. You can update until the winners are announced.

06

Breaks the rules?

Not 1:64, AI-made, not their own shot, or not following RCMI & sponsors: tap Disqualify and give the reason. The participant is told. Reinstate undoes it.
